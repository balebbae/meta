# User research: why fitness is how young adults make friends

Published research, cited. We have not run our own survey yet; `Survey` below is the 2-minute form to send friends for first-hand numbers.

## The numbers

| Finding | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Young adults are the loneliest age group worldwide | 27% of 19-29 year olds feel very or fairly lonely, vs 17% of people 65+ | [Meta-Gallup, Global State of Social Connections, 142 countries (2023)](https://news.gallup.com/opinion/gallup/512618/almost-quarter-world-feels-lonely.aspx) |
| About half of U.S. adults report loneliness, with some of the highest rates among young adults | ~50% | [U.S. Surgeon General advisory, Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation (2023)](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) |
| 15-24 year olds spend far less time with friends than they used to | 70% drop in time with friends over two decades | [AP on the Surgeon General advisory (2023)](https://apnews.com/article/surgeon-general-loneliness-334450f7bb5a77e88d8085b178340e19) |
| Gen Z uses fitness groups to make friends | 66% of Gen Z made new friends through a fitness group (58% overall) | [Strava Year in Sport 2024, survey of 5,068 active people](https://www.runnersworld.com/news/a63116118/strava-year-in-sport-2024/) |
| Social connection is the main reason Gen Z joins | 55% of Gen Z joined a fitness group mainly to make social connections (48% overall) | same |
| Fitness is replacing the bar | 1 in 5 Gen Z went on a date with someone they met exercising; 4x more likely to want to meet people working out than at a bar | [Strava press release](https://press.strava.com/ea/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend) |
| Run clubs are booming | +59% running clubs on Strava in a year | same |
| People do more together | Group activities (10+ people) are 40% longer than solo ones | same |
| Even the most active age group mostly isn't active enough | Only 54.0% of 18-34 year olds meet the aerobic activity guideline | [CDC NCHS Data Brief 555, NHIS 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db555.htm) |
| Strength work is rarer still | 24.2% of adults meet both aerobic and muscle-strengthening guidelines | [CDC FastStats](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/fastats/exercise.htm) |

## What it means for Activities
- Loneliness peaks in exactly our users' age band, and young adults already treat workouts as the place to meet people. Activities puts that inside the app where their friends already are.
- Groups go 40% longer than solo sessions, which is why games are group-first (Rally boss fights, Turf, Mirror Match).
- Almost half of 18-34s miss the activity guideline, so the game has to welcome beginners: handicaps scale the goal so different levels play together.

## Survey (send to friends, 2 minutes)
1. Age: 18-21 / 22-25 / 26-29 / 30+
2. In the last month, how many times did you work out with someone else? 0 / 1-2 / 3-5 / 6+
3. Have you made a friend through a workout, run club or sports team? Yes / No
4. How many times has a group chat said "we should work out together" and it never happened? Never / Once / A few times / All the time
5. Would you join a weekly team fitness challenge with friends inside Facebook / Messenger? Yes / Maybe / No
6. Which would you share with friends in a game? Just that I worked out / Distance or time / Route map / Heart rate / None
7. What's the main thing that stops you from working out with friends? (free text)
